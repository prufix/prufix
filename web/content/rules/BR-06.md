## Definition

BT-27 is the seller's registered legal name. It is easy to satisfy this rule
by accident with the *wrong* element, because UBL has two similarly-named
party name fields, and only one of them counts.

## Common causes

- **Populating `cac:PartyName/cbc:Name` (the trading name, BT-28) and
  believing it satisfies BT-27.** It does not. BT-27, the legal name that
  this rule checks, is `cac:PartyLegalEntity/cbc:RegistrationName`. A seller
  that only trades under a brand name different from its registered name
  (e.g. "Acme Coffee" trading as the legal entity "Acme Holdings B.V.") must
  populate both fields separately, but the legal one is the mandatory one.
- **`cac:PartyLegalEntity` present with a company ID/registration number but
  no `RegistrationName` child** — the legal-entity block was built around
  the registration ID (BT-30) and the name inside the same block was
  overlooked.
- **CII: name placed on the wrong trade party.** `ram:Name` must be a direct
  child of `ram:SellerTradeParty` under
  `ram:ApplicableHeaderTradeAgreement`, not nested under a
  `SpecifiedLegalOrganization` sub-block only.

## How to fix it

- **UBL**: add `cac:PartyLegalEntity/cbc:RegistrationName` under
  `cac:AccountingSupplierParty/cac:Party`. If a trading name is also shown,
  keep it separately in `cac:PartyName/cbc:Name` (BT-28) — don't merge the
  two or drop the legal one.
- **CII**: add `ram:Name` directly under
  `ram:ApplicableHeaderTradeAgreement/ram:SellerTradeParty`.

## Example

### UBL — wrong

```xml
<cac:AccountingSupplierParty>
  <cac:Party>
    <cac:PartyName>
      <cbc:Name>Acme Coffee</cbc:Name>  <!-- trading name only: BT-28, not BT-27 -->
    </cac:PartyName>
  </cac:Party>
</cac:AccountingSupplierParty>
```

### UBL — correct

```xml
<cac:AccountingSupplierParty>
  <cac:Party>
    <cac:PartyName>
      <cbc:Name>Acme Coffee</cbc:Name>
    </cac:PartyName>
    <cac:PartyLegalEntity>
      <cbc:RegistrationName>Acme Holdings B.V.</cbc:RegistrationName>
    </cac:PartyLegalEntity>
  </cac:Party>
</cac:AccountingSupplierParty>
```

### CII — correct

```xml
<ram:SellerTradeParty>
  <ram:Name>Acme Holdings B.V.</ram:Name>
</ram:SellerTradeParty>
```
